# Cooptech (CTVC)

CTVC is a cooperative of technology professionals and startups in Viana do Castelo, Portugal, active since 2021. Together, we share resources, knowledge, and opportunities.

We function as a platform where researchers, developers and other professionals share infrastructure, administration and knowledge, and develop R&D and software projects together. Decisions are made democratically in assemblies where all members have the right to vote.

A CTVC é uma cooperativa de profissionais e startups de tecnologia em Viana do Castelo. Juntos, partilhamos recursos, conhecimento e oportunidades.
